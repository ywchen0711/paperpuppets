title: 卡在樹上的風箏 | The Kite in the Tree

== park day | 公園・午後
[leo enters left to 0.42 running holding kite]
leo (excited): 飛起來！快飛起來呀！ | Fly! Come on, fly!
[kite to tree]
leo (surprised): 啊！不要啊—— | Oh no! Nooo—
[leo runs to 0.55]
[leo looks at kite]
leo (sad): 我的風箏……卡在樹上了。 | My kite... it's stuck in the tree.
[leo jumps]
[leo jumps]
leo (sad): 嗚……太高了。 | Ugh... it's too high.
[mia enters right to 0.8]
mia (thinking): 你在做什麼？在跳舞嗎？ | What are you doing? Dancing?
[leo faces mia]
leo (shy): 不、不是啦……我的風箏飛到樹上去了。 | N-no... my kite flew up into the tree.
[mia looks at kite]
mia (surprised): 哇，好高喔！ | Whoa, that's high!
[mia walks to 0.74]
mia (happy): 我叫小米，就住在那邊。你是新搬來的嗎？ | I'm Mia, I live right over there. Are you new here?
leo (shy): 嗯，我叫阿樂，昨天才搬來。 | Yeah. I'm Leo. We just moved in yesterday.
mia (excited): 好！阿樂，我們一起把它拿下來！ | OK, Leo! Let's get it down together!
[mia walks to 0.7]
[mia jumps]
[mia jumps]
mia (sad): 差一點點……還是搆不到。 | So close... still can't reach.
leo (thinking): 如果有一根長長的東西就好了。 | If only we had something long.
[grandma enters right to 0.86 holding cane]
grandma (happy): 小米——吃點心囉！ | Mia! Snack time!
mia (surprised): 奶奶！ | Grandma!
[mia runs to 0.76]
mia (happy): 奶奶，你的拐杖借我們用一下好不好？ | Grandma, can we borrow your walking stick?
grandma (thinking): 拐杖？你們要做什麼呀？ | My stick? Whatever for?
[mia points at kite]
leo (shy): 我的風箏卡在樹上了…… | My kite's stuck in the tree...
grandma (laugh): 呵呵，這個簡單，交給奶奶。 | Ho ho, that's easy. Leave it to Grandma.
[grandma walks to 0.7]
[grandma reaches up]
[kite falls to 0.6]
leo (excited): 下來了！ | It came down!
[leo holds kite]
mia (laugh): 奶奶好厲害！ | Grandma, you're amazing!
[leo bows]
leo (happy): 謝謝奶奶！ | Thank you, Grandma!
grandma (happy): 不客氣。先去吃塊餅乾，等一下再去放風箏。 | You're welcome. Have a cookie first, then go fly it.
[mia walks to 0.24; leo walks to 0.33]
[mia sits; leo sits]
mia (happy): 阿樂，明天也一起玩吧！ | Leo, let's play again tomorrow!
leo (excited): 嗯！一言為定！ | Yeah! It's a deal!
